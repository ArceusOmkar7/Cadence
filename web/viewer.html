Calibration

Saved on the glove. Do steps 1–3 once after fitting the glove (step 4 sets the palm pressure scale); repeat if it slips. Each step starts after a 3 s countdown and then holds for 3 s.

Heading any time

Hold the hand flat, pointing where you want “forward” (for example at the screen), then press Reset heading.

Advanced: accelerometer 6-point calibration

Removes accelerometer offset and scale error (tilt accuracy). Hold the motion sensor still with each axis pointing straight up, then straight down, using the axis arrows on the board. Redo steps 1 and 2 afterwards.

Model

Pose is streamed over WebSocket (port 81) at about 30 Hz. Finger joint angles are estimated from one bend sensor per finger with fixed joint coupling; finger spread and thumb opposition are not measured.
